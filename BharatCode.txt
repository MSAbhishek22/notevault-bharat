NoteVault Bharat — built for the BharatCode Build League.

Ask your own notes, in your language, answered by the BharatCode
model API (qwen-3.8-27b by default, deepseek-v4.1-flash fallback).

to the API, only when you ask. Grounded answers with citations,
visible routing + budget, and a 3D note constellation.
