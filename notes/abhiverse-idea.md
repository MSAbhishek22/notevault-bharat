# Abhiverse idea

Your phone is the remote for your laptop.

Abhiverse lets a phone control a laptop: power actions, remote view, and
file transfer, with approval-gated input instead of an always-open
account login. The laptop must be on, with the agent running, for
remote control to work — a powered-off laptop cannot be woken from
elsewhere, the same limitation as Phone Link.
